# Site Repositioning Summary

## Old Positioning

Vibepreneur was positioned as a **structured tool** for turning expertise into scalable products. The framing was tool-led: "insight engine," "solution builder," "positioning studio." The site communicated what the product _does_ rather than what the user's business _looks like in motion_.

## New Positioning

Vibepreneur is now positioned as a **venture operating system** for professionals turning expertise into ownership. The framing is system-led: six operating layers that take a user from background to operating business. The site communicates _execution in motion_, not just feature descriptions.

**Core message:** Your experience is already a business. We make it operational.

## Why the Architecture Changed

1. **Category creation**: "Venture OS" creates a new mental model distinct from generic AI builders, no-code tools, and startup courses.
2. **Execution tangibility**: The old site described capabilities. The new site shows work happening (execution cockpit, daily briefs, activity feeds).
3. **Audience specificity**: Four persona landing pages address distinct motivations rather than generic "professionals."
4. **Trust building**: "What You Own" section and transparent roadmap phases build credibility with skeptical professionals.
5. **Conversion architecture**: Multi-step waitlist flow with preview cards replaces a generic form.
